About the Story

This game is a contemporary interactive interpretation of the Act of Patan from the Pabuji epic.

The traditional Phad tradition combines painting, oral storytelling, music and performance.

Gameplay elements have been creatively adapted for this interactive experience and should not be taken as direct historical documentation.

Pabuji ki Phad

Pabuji is a legendary hero-deity of Rajasthan, revered especially among pastoral communities. His legend is told through the Phad, a large painted cloth scroll used by the Bhopa priest-performers as a backdrop for epic narration accompanied by the Rāvaṇhattā fiddle.

The Phad Tradition

Phad paintings are created by the Joshi family of painters in Bhilwara. The paintings use bold outlines and flat colours, depicting multiple episodes of the hero's life simultaneously across the scroll.

Kesar Kalami

The black mare Kesar Kalami is central to Pabuji's story. Her acquisition and the obligations it created form a key narrative thread in the epic.

Creative Interpretation

This game creatively adapts the Act of Patan for an interactive medium. Gameplay elements, enemy classes, and specific combat sequences are inventions of this project and not documented events from the traditional epic.
